About / 2026

I like the part users never see.

I’m Adam Harith J — a Diploma in Computer Science graduate from UiTM Kedah, now studying Computer Networks at UiTM Jasin. I’ve built a web reservation system, mobile apps, and a prayer companion during an internship at FELDA.

I build with what I’m learning — web apps in PHP and MySQL, mobile apps in Flutter and Dart, data layers in Firebase and Hive. The part I enjoy most is the part users never see: the schema, the flow, the model behind the screen.

Now I’m studying how data actually moves — how systems stay connected, and how they fail. I’d rather understand something deeply than pretend to know everything.
