2025 — 2026 · Mobile · FELDA internship

Waqafer

Role
Mobile app developer (intern) — Flutter implementation
Stack
Flutter · Dart · Google Maps
Context
Internship at FELDA, Sep 2025 – Feb 2026
Source
github.com/adamnymy/waqafer
Release
Google Play
01

Problem

Daily Muslim practice scatters across several apps: prayer times in one, qiblah in another, Quran in a third. FELDA needed one companion app for its community that covers the routine — prayer scheduling, direction-finding, and Islamic content.

02

Approach

I built the app in Flutter with Dart: prayer times, a qiblah compass, nearby mosques on Google Maps, plus Quran and Hadith reading and waqf features. My focus was making the daily loop — check time, find direction, read — quick and legible in one place.

03

Outcome

Waqafer is published on Google Play — my first production mobile release, built through the full internship cycle from feature work to store delivery.
