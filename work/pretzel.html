2025 · Mobile · Marketplace

Pretzel

Role
Sole developer — app design, Firebase model, code
Stack
Flutter · Dart · Firebase
Context
Personal project for students
Source
github.com/adamnymy/pretzel_flutter
01

Problem

Course textbooks are expensive, and selling them on is clumsier than buying them: group chats and notice boards with no listing, no record, no way to agree a handover. Students needed something purpose-built instead of a workaround.

02

Approach

I structured the whole thing on Firebase — accounts and data on Cloud Firestore, and the app in Flutter. The loop I optimized for was list a book, chat with the seller, track it on a wishlist, and complete a checkout.

03

Outcome

A working marketplace where the agreement between buyer and seller lives inside the app. It doubled as my Firebase grounding — every feature since in Flutter starts from what this project taught me about modelling realtime data.
